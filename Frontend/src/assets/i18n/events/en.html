Event and Activity Organization

Join the volunteer team of our shelter!

Our shelter gives dozens of animals a chance at a new life every day—animals who have found themselves without a home and care. We do everything possible to provide them with food, medical assistance, and a safe space. But in order to continue growing and attracting more compassionate people, we need your help.

If you have experience in organizing events, creative ideas, or simply want to try yourself in a new role, we will be truly glad to have you in our team!

Here everyone can realize their own talent: some are good at organizing processes, some know how to find partners and sponsors, and some can share their creativity to create a warm atmosphere.

Together, we can achieve more: draw attention to the problems of stray animals, raise funds for their treatment and nutrition, and find new friends and families for our wards.

Join us! Share your ideas, experience, or simply your willingness to help. We will support every step and help turn your plans into reality.
