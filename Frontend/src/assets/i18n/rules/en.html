Dobrodiy Shelter Privacy Policy

THIS PRIVACY POLICY DESCRIBES WHAT PERSONAL DATA WE COLLECT, HOW WE USE, STORE, AND PROTECT IT, AND WHAT RIGHTS USERS HAVE REGARDING THEIR DATA. IT APPLIES TO ALL USERS OF THE DOBRODIY WEBSITE AND APPLICATION.

  2. How we use your data

  4. Data sharing
  5. Your rights
  6. Changes to the policy

  1. What data we collect
      We may collect the following information:
    • First name, last name, or nickname
    • Email address
    • Phone number
    • Account information (username, password)
    • Selected role (adopter, volunteer, donor)
    • Preferences (e.g., saved animals, application history)
    • Communication with administration (requests, messages)
    • Technical device data: type, IP address, OS version, etc.
  2. How we use your data
    • Creating and managing your account
    • Processing adoption, volunteer, or donation requests
    • Sending important information (e.g., request status)
    • Improving app functionality and user experience
    • Sending news updates, with your consent
  3. Data storage and protection
    • All personal data is stored encrypted on secure servers
    • We apply technical and organizational measures to prevent unauthorized access
    • Access to personal data is granted only to authorized shelter staff or technical support
  4. Data sharing

    We do not share your personal data with third parties, except as required by law or with your explicit consent.

  5. Your rights
      You have the right to:
    • Access your personal data
    • Correct or update it
    • Delete your account and all stored data
    • Opt out of informational messages

      • To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information below.

  6. Changes to the policy

    We may periodically update this policy. All changes are published on this page, and the effective date is listed above.
