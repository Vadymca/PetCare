Public Offer

    This Public Offer for making a charitable donation (hereinafter – the Offer) is addressed to an undefined circle of individuals (hereinafter – the Donor) and constitutes a public proposal by Dobrodiy (hereinafter – Dobrodiy) to enter into an agreement regarding making a charitable donation, under the terms set out below:

  1. Concepts and Definitions Used in the Agreement

    Public Offer (and/or the Offer) – a valid proposal by Dobrodiy posted on the website: {{ siteName }}, regarding making a charitable donation, addressed to an undefined circle of individuals.

    Acceptance – full and unconditional acceptance of the Offer by performing actions aimed at transferring funds using payment forms and methods, both posted on the Dobrodiy's website and by transferring money to the Dobrodiy’s current account through banking institutions. The Offer is considered accepted from the date funds are credited to the Dobrodiy’s current account.

    Charitable donation – a gratuitous transfer by the Donor of funds to the Dobrodiy for further use to achieve the Dobrodiy's objectives, as provided by its Charter or programs, in accordance with the Law of Ukraine “On Charitable Activities and Charitable Organizations”, the Dobrodiy’s Charter, and this Agreement.

    Donor – a legally capable individual who voluntarily engages in one or more types of charitable activity. For the purposes of this Agreement, the Donor is the individual who has accepted the Offer.

  2. Subject of the Agreement

    The subject of this Agreement is the gratuitous and voluntary transfer by the Donor of funds to the Dobrodiy’s ownership through a charitable donation to support the Dobrodiy's statutory activities, including (but not limited to) providing charitable assistance under the Law of Ukraine “On Charitable Activities and Charitable Organizations”, the Dobrodiy's programs, etc. The Donor independently determines the amount and directions of the charitable donation.

    The performance of this Agreement by the parties is not intended to generate profit or benefits for any party.

  3. Acceptance

    By accepting the Offer, the Donor confirms that they agree with all terms of the Offer and understand and consent that the donation will be used to achieve the Dobrodiy's objectives, as provided by its Charter, which they may review by sending a request to the email address hello@catdog.com.ua Furthermore, by accepting the Offer, the Donor fully understands and agrees with the subject of the Agreement, objectives, and purpose of the public fundraising, and confirms the Dobrodiy's right to use part of the charitable donation for administrative expenses in an amount not exceeding that provided by the Law of Ukraine “On Charitable Activities and Charitable Organizations” or other legal acts.

    The parties agree that from the moment the Offer is accepted, this Agreement is concluded in writing in accordance with Articles 207, 639, 641, and 642 of the Civil Code of Ukraine and Articles 6 and 7 of the Law of Ukraine “On Charitable Activities and Charitable Organizations”. At the same time, the parties agree that non-execution of this Agreement as a separate document does not invalidate it.

  4. Rights and Obligations of the Dobrodiy

      The Dobrodiy has the right:
    • to receive charitable donations and use them in accordance with the subject and terms of this Agreement and its statutory activities;
    • to change the directions of charitable donation usage with the Donor's consent;
    • to use part of the charitable donation for administrative expenses in an amount not exceeding that provided by the Law of Ukraine “On Charitable Activities and Charitable Organizations” or other legal acts;
    • to change donation usage directions within statutory activities without the Donor's consent;
      The Dobrodiy undertakes:
    • to report annually, in electronic form, on the use of charitable donations by posting the relevant information on the website: {{ siteName }}/reports;
    • to use received donations solely to achieve the objectives provided by the Dobrodiy’s Charter

  5. Rights of the Donor

    The Donor has the right to monitor the use of charitable donations by the Dobrodiy for their intended purposes.

  6. Location of Public Fundraising

    Public fundraising of charitable donations is carried out in any country worldwide. The Dobrodiy's activities aimed at achieving its objectives are carried out on the territory of Ukraine (excluding temporarily occupied territories and areas of anti-terrorist operations).

  7. Fundraising Period

    The public fundraising continues until the termination of the Dobrodiy's activity (including liquidation), unless another period is set by the Dobrodiy, about which the Donor will be notified by posting the relevant information on the website: {{ siteName }}

  8. Procedure for Using Charitable Donations

    The use of charitable donations is carried out in accordance with the Dobrodiy's statutory activities and the current legislation of Ukraine, including the Law of Ukraine “On Charitable Activities and Charitable Organizations” and other legal acts. The Dobrodiy uses donations according to its statutory activities and programs. Received charitable donations may only be returned to the Donor in cases provided by the legislation of Ukraine and this Agreement.

    In the event that the project for which the Dobrodiy collected donations is not implemented, the Dobrodiy changes the direction of donation usage by transferring such funds to another project within its statutory activities.

  9. Liability of the Dobrodiy

    The Dobrodiy is responsible for violating the terms of this Agreement and using charitable donations contrary to the procedures provided by its statutory activities and Ukrainian legislation, in accordance with current Ukrainian law.

  10. General Access to Dobrodiy Information

    The Dobrodiy's financial statements are disclosed by posting on the Dobrodiy's website: {{ siteName }}/reports, annually by March 1 of the year following the reporting year. Other information is disclosed by the Dobrodiy in accordance with the procedure and volumes provided by the legislation of Ukraine.

  11. Expenses related to transferring charitable donations (transfer fees, taxes, charges, etc.) are borne by the Donor. By accepting the Offer, the Donor confirms that they have read the Agreement and consent to the collection, processing, and use of personal data in accordance with applicable legislation of Ukraine.
